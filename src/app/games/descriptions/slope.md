### What Is Slope?

**Slope** is a fast-paced endless runner browser game where you control a rolling ball as it speeds down a narrow, floating track. The goal is simple: survive as long as possible without falling off the edge or crashing into red obstacles. The further you go, the faster the game becomes, pushing your reaction time and control skills to the limit.

Because Slope runs directly in the browser and doesn’t require downloads, it’s become one of the most popular games to play at school or on low-end devices.



### How to Play Slope Online

To play **Slope online**, all you need is a keyboard and quick reflexes.

* Use the **A / D keys** or **Left / Right arrow keys** to steer the ball
* Avoid **red blocks**, which instantly end your run
* Stay centered on the track to avoid slipping off the sides

The game never stops or pauses — once you start, your only objective is to keep going and beat your previous high score.



### Tips to Get a Higher Score in Slope

If you’re struggling to last more than a few seconds, these tips can help:

* **Make small movements** instead of sharp turns
* **Look ahead**, not directly at the ball
* Stay calm when the speed increases — panicking causes oversteering
* Practice controlling the ball near the center of the track

Slope rewards consistency more than risky moves. The longer you survive, the higher your score climbs.
