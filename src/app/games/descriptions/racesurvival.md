**Race Survival** is a chaotic driving game where staying alive matters more than finishing first. Players control a car on dangerous tracks filled with gaps, moving platforms, and sudden drops.

The goal is to avoid falling off while other cars crash around you. Quick reactions and smart positioning are key as the track constantly changes and removes sections beneath players.

Race Survival is perfect for players who enjoy fast-paced arcade racing with unpredictable outcomes and high tension.
