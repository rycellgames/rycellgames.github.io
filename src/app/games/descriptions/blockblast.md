game: "blockblast"

**Block Blast** is a relaxing yet strategic puzzle game where players place colorful block shapes onto a grid to clear lines and score points. Unlike traditional falling-block games, you can place blocks at your own pace, making careful planning more important than quick reactions.

The goal is to clear as many rows and columns as possible without running out of space. As the board fills up, each decision matters, rewarding players who think ahead and manage the grid efficiently.

With simple controls, bright visuals, and endless gameplay, Block Blast is perfect for casual players who enjoy satisfying puzzle games that are easy to learn but challenging to master.
