game: "cookieclicker"

**Cookie Clicker** is an incremental idle game where the goal is to produce as many cookies as possible. You start by clicking a cookie to earn cookies, then spend them on upgrades and buildings that automate production.

As numbers grow, new mechanics, upgrades, and strategies unlock, turning a simple clicking game into a complex optimization challenge. The game runs even while idle, rewarding long-term progression.

Cookie Clicker is perfect for players who enjoy idle games, large numbers, and gradual progression with minimal effort.
