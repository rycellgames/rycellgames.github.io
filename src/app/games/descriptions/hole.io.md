**Hole.io** is a fast-paced arcade game where you control a growing hole that swallows everything in its path. Starting small, you consume objects like street signs, cars, and buildings to increase your size.

The game is often played against other holes, adding a competitive element as players race to grow larger within a time limit. Strategy matters, as you must choose what to eat and when to avoid larger opponents.

With simple controls and chaotic gameplay, Hole.io is perfect for quick, competitive sessions and casual fun.
