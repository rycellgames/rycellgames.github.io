**Drift Hunters** is a 3D car drifting game focused on skill, control, and customization. Players earn points by performing drifts and chaining them together for higher scores.

The game features multiple cars, upgrade options, and open tracks designed specifically for drifting. Smooth controls and realistic physics make mastering each vehicle feel rewarding.

Drift Hunters is a popular choice for players who enjoy driving games, car customization, and skill-based racing mechanics.
