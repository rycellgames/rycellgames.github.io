**Drift Boss** is an addictive one-button drifting game that challenges your timing and control. You drive a car along a narrow, floating track filled with sharp corners and sudden turns, where one mistake sends you falling into the void. The car moves forward automatically, and your only control is when to drift — simple to learn, but hard to master.

To play, **click and hold (or press the spacebar)** to drift right, and **release** to drift left. The goal is to stay on the road for as long as possible and travel the furthest distance without falling off. There’s no finish line, only an endless track that becomes more difficult the further you go.

Drift Boss is perfect for quick play sessions, but its challenging gameplay makes it easy to keep coming back for “just one more try.” With fast restarts, smooth controls, and increasing difficulty, it’s a popular choice for players who enjoy skill-based arcade driving games.
