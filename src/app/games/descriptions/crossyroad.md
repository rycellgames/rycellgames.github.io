**Crossy Road** is a fast-paced arcade game inspired by classic endless runners, where the goal is simple: cross the road without getting hit. You control a small character and guide them across busy roads, rivers, train tracks, and other hazards while trying to survive as long as possible.

The controls are easy to learn. **Tap or click to move forward**, **swipe or use arrow keys** to move sideways, and time your movements carefully to avoid cars, trains, and other obstacles. The screen constantly scrolls forward, so stopping for too long will end your run.

Each run is different, with randomly generated levels that keep the gameplay fresh. As you play, you can unlock a wide variety of characters, each with unique designs and small visual effects.

Crossy Road is known for its colorful blocky art style, simple controls, and addictive gameplay loop. It’s perfect for quick sessions, but challenging enough to keep players coming back to beat their high score.
