**Tiny Fishing** is a relaxing idle fishing game where the goal is to catch fish, earn money, and upgrade your gear. You cast your line, move it left and right to catch fish, and earn coins based on what you reel in.

Upgrades allow you to fish deeper, catch more valuable species, and increase your earnings over time. The game blends active play with idle progression, making it easy to enjoy in short sessions.

With colorful visuals and simple mechanics, Tiny Fishing is perfect for casual players who enjoy progression-based games with low pressure.
