**World’s Hardest Game** is a notoriously difficult puzzle-action game that tests patience, timing, and precision. You guide a small square through maze-like levels while avoiding moving obstacles and collecting required items.

Controls are simple, using arrow keys to move, but the patterns of enemies are unforgiving. Every level requires careful planning and perfect execution, and even one mistake sends you back to the start.

Despite its difficulty, the game is fair and pattern-based, rewarding players who learn enemy movements. World’s Hardest Game is a classic choice for players who enjoy extreme challenge and high-skill gameplay.
