**Idle Breakout** is an incremental idle game inspired by classic brick-breaking arcade games. Balls automatically bounce around the screen, breaking blocks and earning money over time.

Players can purchase upgrades, unlock new types of balls, and increase damage to break blocks faster. Even when you’re not actively playing, progress continues, making it easy to return and see big improvements.

Idle Breakout combines idle mechanics with satisfying visuals and steady progression, appealing to players who enjoy automation and long-term growth.
