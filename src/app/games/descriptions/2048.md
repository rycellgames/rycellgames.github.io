game: "2048"
---

**2048** is a classic number puzzle game where the goal is to combine tiles to reach the 2048 tile. You slide numbered tiles across a grid, merging matching numbers to create higher values.

Each move adds a new tile, forcing players to think ahead and manage space carefully. While the rules are simple, achieving high scores requires strategy and planning.

2048 is perfect for players who enjoy logic puzzles, brain-training games, and calm but challenging gameplay.
