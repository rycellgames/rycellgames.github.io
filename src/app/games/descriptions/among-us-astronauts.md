**Among Us Astronauts** is a multiplayer social deduction game set aboard a spaceship, where players must work together while remaining alert to hidden threats. Each match places players in a group with different roles, some focused on completing objectives and others working secretly to disrupt the mission.

Players move around the map completing tasks, observing behavior, and communicating with others to figure out who can be trusted. Suspicious actions, sudden disruptions, and timed discussions force players to rely on logic, teamwork, and deception.

The goal depends on your role: either help complete all objectives and identify the saboteurs, or secretly eliminate players without being discovered. Every round plays out differently, making communication and strategy just as important as movement.

Among Us Astronauts is ideal for players who enjoy mind games, teamwork, and tense multiplayer experiences built around trust and betrayal.
