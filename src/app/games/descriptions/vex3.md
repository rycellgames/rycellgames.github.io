**Vex 3** is a fast-paced platformer that focuses on precise movement and sharp reflexes. You control a stickman character as they run, jump, slide, and wall-climb through dangerous obstacle-filled levels. Each stage is packed with spikes, saw blades, moving platforms, and traps that punish even small mistakes.

The controls are responsive and simple, but the levels demand perfect timing. Checkpoints are placed throughout the map, encouraging trial-and-error gameplay while keeping frustration low. As you progress, levels become more complex and require mastery of momentum and parkour-style movement.

Vex 3 is ideal for players who enjoy challenging skill-based platform games and speedrunning-style gameplay.
