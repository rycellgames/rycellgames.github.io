game: "bloxorz"
---

**Bloxors** is a physics-based puzzle game where you control a rectangular block and roll it across floating platforms to reach a goal hole. The challenge comes from managing the block’s orientation, as it can stand upright or lie flat depending on how you move it.

Players must carefully plan each move to avoid falling off the platform or getting stuck. Later levels introduce switches, fragile tiles, and split blocks, adding depth and complexity to the puzzles.

Bloxors is ideal for players who enjoy logic puzzles, spatial reasoning, and slow, thoughtful gameplay.
