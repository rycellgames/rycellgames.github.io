**Slither** is a modern multiplayer snake game where players control a snake that grows longer by consuming glowing orbs. The challenge is avoiding collisions with other players while trying to trap them into crashing.

As your snake grows, movement becomes riskier, turning each decision into a balance between aggression and survival. One wrong move instantly ends the run.

Slither is easy to pick up but highly competitive, making it a popular choice for players who enjoy simple mechanics with high skill potential.
